# About Me

I'm passionate about exploring how bioinformatics, data science, and machine learning can help us better understand complex biological systems and advance biomedical research.

- 🔬 Research Interests

- 🧬 Genomics, Transcriptomics & Proteomics

- 🔬 Multi-Omics Data Analysis & Integration

- 💊 Drug Discovery & Computational Pharmacology

- 🤖 Machine Learning & Biomedical Data Science

- 🦠 Immunology & Molecular Biology

I'm particularly interested in applying computational methods to biological and clinical data, uncovering meaningful patterns, and contributing to research that can improve our understanding of diseases and support the development of new therapeutic approaches.

📍 Based in Odense, Denmark.
